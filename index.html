📖 Methodology & Reference Equations
Gulf Guide AMR 2025 · Tables 26–30 · WHO ATC/DDD Index 2025
Table 26 — Antimicrobial Consumption DDD/1000PD = (Vials × Strength ÷ WHO DDD ÷ Patient-Days) × 1,000
DOT/1000PD = (Antimicrobial Days ÷ Patient-Days) × 1,000
DDD:DOT Ratio = DDD/1000PD ÷ DOT/1000PD · [>1.2 above-standard · 0.8–1.2 standard · <0.8 shorter]

Table 27 — AMR Resistance Rates E. coli Resistance (%) = (Resistant E. coli ÷ Total E. coli isolates) × 100
Klebsiella Resistance (%) = (Resistant Klebsiella ÷ Total Klebsiella isolates) × 100
MRSA Rate (%) = (MRSA isolates ÷ Total S. aureus isolates) × 100

Table 28 — Surgical Antibiotic Prophylaxis Timing Compliance (%) = (Compliant within 60 min pre-op ÷ Total surgical patients) × 100
Choice Compliance (%) = (Guideline-concordant antibiotic ÷ Total surgical patients) × 100
Stopping Compliance (%) = (Stopped ≤24h post-op ÷ Total surgical patients) × 100 · Target: ≥80%

Table 29 — Infection Rates MRSA Bacteraemia = (Hospital-onset MRSA events ÷ Patient-Days) × 1,000
MDRO Incidence = (Hospital-onset MDRO events ÷ Patient-Days) × 10,000
CDI Rate = (Hospital-onset CDI cases ÷ Patient-Days) × 1,000
SSI Rate (%) = (SSI events ÷ Total Surgical Procedures) × 100

Table 30 — Quality Outcomes Hospital LOS (days) = Total Bed-Days ÷ Total Admissions
Hospital Mortality (%) = (In-Hospital Deaths ÷ Total Admissions) × 100
Sepsis Mortality (%) = (Sepsis Deaths ÷ Total Sepsis Cases) × 100
30-Day Readmission (%) = (Readmissions ≤30d ÷ Total Discharges) × 100
⚠ CAZ-AVI: WHO DDD 6,000 mg = ceftazidime component only · Use 2,000 mg/vial (Option B, WHO-aligned).
